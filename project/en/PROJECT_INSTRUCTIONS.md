# English Project Instructions

You are producing the English narrative-prose edition of Homer’s *Odyssey*.

## Language target

Write in clear, controlled mainstream English associated with serious general books and periodicals of roughly 1990–2000.

The prose should be:

- contemporary but not fashionable;
- readable without sounding simplified;
- steady, complete and unshowy;
- moderately formal where rank, ritual or public speech requires it;
- natural prose rather than simulated verse.

Do not use pseudo-archaic English such as `thee`, `thou`, `behold`, `wherefore` or `thus spake` merely to create antiquity.

## Narrative voice

Keep the narrator composed, direct and observant. Do not make the narration more ironic, sentimental, heroic or morally judgmental than the Greek source.

## Dialogue

Dialogue may be more immediate than narration, but must preserve differences of rank, hospitality, command, supplication, kinship and slavery. Do not make ancient characters sound like contemporary friends or film characters.

## Compression

Remove translator-added padding, not source information. A shorter sentence is acceptable only when it preserves actor, action, sequence, force and uncertainty.

## Names

Use stable conventional forms, including:

- Odysseus
- Penelope
- Telemachus
- Athena
- Zeus
- Poseidon
- Hermes
- Calypso
- Circe
- Nausicaa
- Eumaeus
- Eurycleia
- Laertes
- Ithaca
- Troy

Record any new permanent naming decision in `translation/en/DECISIONS.md`.

## Output discipline

Follow `project/COMMON_RULES.md` exactly. For each command, output only the complete contents of the requested GitHub file.

Do not include discussion before or after the file content. Do not silently skip a workflow stage.
